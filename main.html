📖 My Full Story

My Journey

Before — 108 kg

After — 70 kg

It all started way back in 2011 when my elder daughter was less than a year old. I realized I couldn't even run behind her without losing breath. That day, I decided to get back to fitness. Once a college fitness enthusiast, years of corporate life had pushed me over 108 kg. That moment reignited my passion for health and movement.

I started small — learning swimming, cycling, playing badminton — just trying to move again. In 2013, a friend introduced me to a running group at Cubbon Park. My wife, daughter, and I would join every weekend — she'd stroll in the park while I ran with the group. It wasn't easy at first, but step by step, I began to find my rhythm again.

Since then, I've taken part in countless 5Ks, 10Ks, half-marathons, full-marathons, and even ultras. There were days when I struggled with injuries or self-doubt, but I never stopped showing up. As of today, I've completed 8 full marathons, achieving a personal best of 3:39 in 2022.

Through this journey, I've learned that transformation isn't about one big change — it's about the small daily habits, consistency, environment, and the people who lift you up.

I've lived that journey — and now, I help others find their own. Whether you're starting from scratch or chasing your next PR, I can guide you to build strength, endurance, and a mindset that lasts.

🏃 "Fitness isn't a destination — it's who you become on the way."
